The Master Plan

Must-Do's & Eats

Phu Quoc Activity

Hon Thom Cable Car

The longest non-stop three-rope cable car in the world. 15 minutes of gliding over turquoise waters and fishing villages down in the south of the island.

Phu Quoc Local Eats

Bún Quậy (Stirred Noodles)

The island's signature dish. Fresh rice noodle soup with shrimp and squid. The trick is making your own dipping sauce with kumquat and chili, and stirring it into the broth.

Dinner Vibes

Duong Dong Night Market

The best place for fresh seafood. Pick sea urchins, scallops, and squid right from the tanks and they'll grill it for you on the spot with scallion oil and peanuts.

Sightseeing

Sunset Town

Located near the cable car station, it looks like a Mediterranean city dropped into Vietnam. Home to the "Kiss Bridge" and great evening entertainment shows.
